# Surrogate Country Selection in Foreign Input Valuation

## Executive Summary

The Department of Commerce selects surrogate country datasets based on economic comparability, production comparability, and input data specificity [[claim:c001]]. In Determination A-570-967, the Department of Commerce rejected a broad 12-country world market average input dataset in favor of a specific 2-country dataset [[claim:c002]].

## Risks and Limitations

Selecting narrow surrogate datasets of 1-3 countries introduces elevated single-market price volatility and exchange rate distortion risk [[claim:c003]].

## Recommendations

Enforce secondary citation cross-checks and strict grounding validation when fewer than three surrogate countries represent the input value basket [[claim:c004]].
