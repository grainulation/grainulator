Where Grainulator fits

A clearer way to check AI work.

Grainulator saves the reasons behind a result, checks what you ask it to check, and helps your agent address any problems it finds.

  1. 01

    Start with your task

    Give your agent the question, background, and goal.

  2. 02

    Check what matters

    Choose a test or review step. See what passed and what needs work.

  3. 03

    Try again with feedback

    Send a failed check back to the agent, with a limit on retries.

What this example proves

The saved record shows Grainulator catching a change after a report was checked. It stops the old check from being used. It does not decide whether the release is safe.

Small local model comparisons do not show a general improvement in accuracy or speed.

To repeat the example from the source code, run node scripts/record-handoff.mjs. The technical contract is in docs/ADAPTERS.md.
