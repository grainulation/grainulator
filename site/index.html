How it works

Keep findings and sources together.

Grainulator stores the findings and source references your agent records, flags gaps, and helps your agent address failed checks.

  1. 01

    Start with your task

    Give your agent the question, background, and goal.

  2. 02

    Check what matters

    Choose a test or review step. See what passed, and check the evidence behind conflicting findings.

  3. 03

    Try again with feedback

    Set up a check-and-retry loop to send failed checks back to your agent, with a limit on attempts.

What this example shows

The saved record shows Grainulator catching a change after a report was checked. It stops the old check from being used. It does not decide whether the release is safe.

Grainulator helps you inspect and check AI work. Evaluations have not established a general improvement in accuracy or speed.

To repeat the example from the source code, run node scripts/record-handoff.mjs. The technical contract is in docs/ADAPTERS.md.
